# FORM CHECKLIST FOR A PILOT ARTEFACT

Run by the `ecriture-de-mission` skill (§5) before any filing — Mission, Decision, capture, handoff, proposal: the guardians make no difference, and the two STOPs of Mission 149 fell on a Decision (report 157, F3). A failing line = no filing. Each line cites what paid for it. The checks specific to a Mission (rubrics of the template) apply only to Missions; the form checks apply to everything.
