# DECISION — ALTERNATE GENRES EVERY TWO MONTHS

_Fictional decision, specific to this example project — see the [overview note](../README.md)._

## Decision

Les Pages Suspendues alternate: one month of fiction, one month of non-fiction, without exception, to keep the club from drifting towards a single genre.

## Reason

Three meetings in a row on fiction had driven away two members interested mainly in non-fiction (noted at the September meeting, see the capture in knowledge/).

## Impact

The [rule for choosing the book of the month](../rules/RULES-2026-09-11-193000-choosing-the-book-of-the-month.md) now applies within the genre imposed by the month.
