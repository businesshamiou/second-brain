# CHOOSING THE BOOK OF THE MONTH

_Fictional rule, specific to this example project — see the [overview note](../README.md)._

Each member of Les Pages Suspendues may propose a title before the 20th of the month. The club votes by show of hands at the following meeting; in case of a tie, the person facilitating that month decides. A book already proposed twice without being chosen is not proposed again for six months.

This rule is specific to this project: it would make no sense elsewhere, so it lives here rather than in `second-brain` — see the boundary test of the [project structure standard](../../../rules/RULES-2026-08-26-142800-project-structure-standard.md), §3.
