# DECISION — DOCTRINAL ARBITRATIONS OF 2026-08-25

## Date

2026-08-25

## Status

`ARBITRATED`

Arbitration: Pilot session of 2026-08-25 (« ok pour la Mission doctrinale, vas-y go » ["ok for the doctrinal Mission, go ahead"], on the four points stated by the Pilot in the same exchange); point 4 re-arbitrated by the Owner on 2026-08-25 after explanation, the French exception being rejected.

## Decision

1. **Revocation.** The oral authorization of 2026-08-24 allowing the Pilot a shell execution bounded to read-only measurement is revoked. Rung 2 of the charter remains the criterion: shell capability implies the Executor role. The need that had motivated it (real timestamping) is covered by the MCP mechanism: filing, `get_file_info`, alignment of the name and of `created_at` before the end of the turn — proven in the session of 2026-08-25 (Missions 051 to 053).
2. **Self-filing.** Every Mission commits its own file and regenerates the indexes as the last steps of its window. Evidence of effectiveness: 041/044/045 required three windows for one close; 050/051/052 one each. Engraved in the Mission versioning rule.
3. **Session references.** The state sheet carries a catalogue section « Références de session » [Session references] (paths + one line), generated by `tools/build-state.sh`: role charter, relay rule, journal tags Decision, links standard, the present Decision. The Pilot contract stays capped at seven lines; the sheet copies no normative content (in line with arbitration 2 of the seven arbitrations of 2026-08-23).
4. **Anglicization of the link vocabulary.** System keywords are in English, without exception: the vocabulary of typed links moves to it. Arbitrated correspondence, aligned on the front matter already in English (`supersedes`, `amends`): `applique` → `applies`, `remplace` → `supersedes`, `amende` → `amends`, `source` → `source` (unchanged), `prescrit par` → `prescribed by`, `voir aussi` → `see also`, `remplacé par` → `superseded by`, `amendé par` → `amended by`. Principle engraved for the future: the system is meant for multilingual use — prose, prompts and titles are localized into the user's language; system keywords (journal tags, link types, status values, front-matter fields) stay fixed in English whatever the working language. The migration of the corpus, of the standard and of the tooling is executed by Mission 054, never on the fly. Rejected: the French exception, considered for a time — a localized exception today becomes one exception per language tomorrow.

## Reason

Three oral debts and one observed contradiction, all earlier than 2026-08-25; an unwritten exception to a safety rule erodes the whole rule; a language contradiction in system keywords blocks any clean internationalization.

## Impact

- Door `open-pilot-shell-authorization` closed: the charter is not modified, the MCP mechanism covers the timestamping need.
- Rule on versioning of Missions and generated outputs amended with a self-filing rule.
- `tools/build-state.sh` carries a new catalogue section « Références de session » [Session references].
- The links standard and its vocabulary are not modified by the present Decision: the migration of the corpus, of the tooling and of the standard itself is executed by Mission 054, never on the fly.

## Important alternatives

- Keep the Pilot's oral authorization without engraving or revoking it: set aside, an unwritten exception to a safety rule erodes the whole rule.
- Permanent French exception to the link vocabulary: set aside by the Owner after explanation — a localized exception today becomes one exception per language tomorrow, contrary to the system's multilingual goal.
